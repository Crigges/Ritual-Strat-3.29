03 · ROUTE

In-Map Route

1 · Boss plaza first Check mid. If a Ritual spawned inside the boss arena, kill all three bosses in the circle. If not, pull the bosses over to the nearest one. Their Tribute carries into every later altar, so don’t pop a single Ritual before it’s done.
2 · Outer ring & altars Clear the grid outward, hitting the remaining Rituals. Take the Eldritch altars with the fattest loot multipliers, and skip the ones that would brick your build.
3 · Ore & caches Mine every ore deposit and crack every Smuggler’s Cache on route. If slow ores (Verisium / Amber) tank your maps-per-hour, leave them in the ground.

Dust +4M dust Dust doubles shipment value up to a 1:1 match, then falls off a cliff. 4M bars + 4M dust ≈ 8M shipment. Don’t over-dust past that.

05 · SIDE HUSTLE

Heist Blueprints

  • Sell fully revealed Blueprints raw on trade. The reveal cost is the value; no need to run the Grand Heist yourself.
  • Price-check before dumping: Blueprints with Experimental Items and Currency or Scarabs are the expensive ones. Everything else goes in the bulk tab.
